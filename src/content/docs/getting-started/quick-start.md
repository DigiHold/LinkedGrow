## Create your account

Head to [linkedgrow.ai/sign-up](https://linkedgrow.ai/sign-up) to create your free account. You have three options:

- **Email and password** - Enter your email and choose a password
- **Sign in with Google** - Use your Google account for one-click signup
- **Sign in with LinkedIn** - Use your LinkedIn account (also connects LinkedIn automatically)

After signing up, you will be taken through a brief onboarding flow that helps you configure the essentials.

## Connect your LinkedIn account

To publish and schedule posts, you need to connect your LinkedIn account:

1. Go to **Settings** from the sidebar
2. Find the **LinkedIn Integration** section
3. Click **Connect LinkedIn**
4. You will be redirected to LinkedIn to authorize the connection
5. Approve the requested permissions (profile access and posting ability)
6. You will be redirected back to LinkedGrow with your account connected

Your LinkedIn profile name and avatar will sync automatically. You can check the connection status anytime in Settings.

## Set up your AI API key

LinkedGrow uses the BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model - you connect your own AI API key for unlimited content generation at cost. Typical monthly cost is $2-4 for most users.

1. Go to **Settings > AI API** from the sidebar
2. Choose a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI, Grok, or Perplexity)
3. Paste your API key from the provider's dashboard
4. Select a model (each provider has a recommended option marked)
5. Click **Save**

If you do not have an API key yet, check our [detailed setup guides](/docs/byok) for step-by-step instructions for each provider.

## Configure your voice settings

Help the AI match your writing style by setting up your voice profile:

1. Go to **Settings** and scroll to the voice training section
2. **Business description** - Describe what your business does in 2-3 sentences
3. **Target audience** - Define who you are writing for (job titles, industries, interests)
4. **Writing tone** - Choose your preferred tone (Professional, Casual, Motivational, etc.)
5. **Sample posts** - Paste 3-5 of your best LinkedIn posts so the AI can learn your style
6. **Never mention** - Add any topics, competitors, or words the AI should avoid

## Create your first post

Now you are ready to generate your first LinkedIn post:

1. Click **Generator** in the sidebar (or the "Generate Post" quick action on the dashboard)
2. Choose a post type (Actionable, Inspiring, Introspective, or Promotional)
3. Select a category and tone, or type a custom topic
4. Click **Generate** - the AI will create 3 post variations
5. Click **Use in Editor** on the one you like best
6. Review and edit the post in the editor
7. Check the **Algorithm Score** to optimize your post
8. Click **Publish Now** to post to LinkedIn, or **Schedule** to set a future date

## Understand the dashboard

Your dashboard is the command center. Here is what you will find:

- **Stats grid** - Total posts, drafts, scheduled posts, and published posts at a glance
- **Quick actions** - Shortcuts to Generate Post, Write Post, Repurpose Content, and Get Ideas
- **Recent posts** - Your last 3 posts with status badges
- **Upcoming schedule** - Your next 3 scheduled posts with publish times
- **API key reminder** - A helpful alert if you have not configured an AI provider yet

The sidebar gives you access to all features: Generator, Editor, Ideas, Hooks, Carousel, Posts library, Calendar, and Settings.
