## What are branding settings?

Branding settings let you define your visual identity within LinkedGrow. Your logo, brand colors, and font choices are applied to carousels and other branded content to maintain a consistent, professional look.

## Setting up your brand

Go to **Settings > Branding** (or find the branding section within Settings).

### Logo

Upload your company or personal brand logo. Supported formats include PNG, JPG, and SVG. Your logo appears on:

- Carousel slides (typically in a corner)
- Exported content and downloads

For best results, use a logo with a transparent background (PNG or SVG) so it blends well with different slide backgrounds.

### Primary color

Set your main brand color using the color picker or entering a hex code (e.g., #0891b2). This color is used for:

- Heading text on carousel slides
- Accent elements and highlights
- CTA buttons and important elements

### Secondary color

Your secondary brand color complements the primary. It is used for:

- Background accents
- Supporting text elements
- Borders and dividers

### Font family

Choose a font that matches your brand identity. LinkedGrow includes Google Fonts, giving you access to hundreds of professional typefaces. Your selected font applies to all text in carousels and branded exports.

Popular choices for LinkedIn content include clean, modern fonts like Inter, DM Sans, Poppins, and Montserrat.

## Where branding appears

Your branding settings are currently applied to:

- **Carousel Generator** - All slides inherit your colors, fonts, and logo placement
- **PDF exports** - Downloaded carousels include your branding
- **Saved carousel templates** - Your brand is preserved in templates you save

## Best practices

- **Keep it simple** - Choose 1-2 colors that represent your brand clearly
- **Test readability** - Make sure your brand colors provide enough contrast for text to be readable
- **Use your brand font consistently** - The same font on LinkedIn carousels as on your website creates brand recognition
- **Upload a high-resolution logo** - A crisp logo looks professional on all screen sizes

## Plan availability

Branding settings are available on the Business plan. Users on other plans can upgrade to access custom branding.
