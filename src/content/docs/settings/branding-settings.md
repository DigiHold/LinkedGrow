## What are branding settings?

Branding settings let you define your visual identity for carousels created in LinkedGrow. Your logo, profile picture, handle, website, and brand colors are applied to carousel slides to maintain a consistent, professional look.

## Where to find branding settings

Branding settings are located inside the **Carousel Generator**, not on the main Settings page. When you open the Carousel Generator, you will find the **Branding** panel in the editor sidebar. This is where you configure all your visual brand elements.

## Branding panel components

### Logo toggle

Enable or disable your logo on carousel slides. When enabled, your uploaded logo appears on slides for brand recognition.

### Profile Picture (Avatar)

Your profile picture is displayed on carousel slides, typically on the first or last slide alongside your name. This helps viewers associate the content with your personal brand.

### Handle

Your social media handle (e.g., your LinkedIn username) is displayed on carousel slides so viewers know who created the content and where to find you.

### Website

Add your website URL to carousel slides. This drives traffic back to your site and reinforces your brand identity.

### Primary color

Set your main brand color using the color picker or entering a hex code (e.g., #0891b2). This color is used for:

- Heading text on carousel slides
- Accent elements and highlights
- CTA buttons and important elements

### Secondary color

Your secondary brand color complements the primary. It is used for:

- Background accents
- Supporting text elements
- Borders and dividers

## Where branding appears

Your branding settings are applied to:

- **Carousel slides** -- All slides inherit your colors, logo, avatar, handle, and website
- **PDF exports** -- Downloaded carousels include your branding
- **Saved carousel templates** -- Your brand is preserved in templates you save

## Best practices

- **Keep it simple** -- Choose 1-2 colors that represent your brand clearly
- **Test readability** -- Make sure your brand colors provide enough contrast for text to be readable
- **Upload a high-resolution logo** -- A crisp logo looks professional on all screen sizes
- **Include your handle** -- This makes it easy for people to find and follow you when your carousel is shared
