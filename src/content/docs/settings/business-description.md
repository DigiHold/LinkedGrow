## What is the business description?

Your business description tells the AI what your business does, who you serve, and what makes you unique. It provides essential context so generated content is relevant to your industry and audience.

## How to set it up

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Find the **Business Description** field
3. Write 2-4 sentences describing your business
4. Click **Save**

## Writing a good description

A strong business description answers three questions:

1. **What do you do?** - Your product, service, or area of expertise
2. **Who do you serve?** - Your target customers or audience
3. **What makes you different?** - Your unique angle or value proposition

### Examples

**SaaS founder:**
"I'm the founder of TaskFlow, a project management tool for remote teams of 10-50 people. We help distributed teams stay aligned without excessive meetings. Our key differentiator is async-first communication built into every workflow."

**Marketing consultant:**
"I help B2B SaaS companies generate qualified leads through LinkedIn organic content and thought leadership. I focus on founder-led marketing strategies for companies with $1M-10M ARR."

**Career coach:**
"I'm a career transition coach specializing in helping mid-career professionals move into tech roles. I combine 15 years of hiring experience with practical job search strategies."

## How the AI uses this

Every time you generate content, the AI references your business description to:

- Focus on topics relevant to your industry
- Use appropriate terminology and jargon
- Highlight your expertise and value proposition
- Tailor examples and case studies to your niche

## Tips

- **Be specific, not generic.** "I help people with marketing" is too vague. "I help B2B SaaS companies with LinkedIn content strategy" gives the AI much more to work with.
- **Update when your business evolves.** If you launch a new product, pivot your focus, or change your target market, update your description.
- **Include numbers when possible.** "15 years of experience" or "helped 200+ clients" adds credibility that the AI can incorporate into your content.
