## What is the Never Mention list?

The Never Mention list is a content filter that prevents the AI from using specific words, names, or topics in your generated content. It is a safeguard that ensures your posts stay on-brand and avoid sensitive subjects.

## How to set it up

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Find the **Never Mention** field
3. Enter the words, names, or topics you want the AI to avoid
4. Click **Save**

You can enter multiple items separated by commas or new lines.

## Common uses

### Competitor names

If you do not want your content to accidentally mention or promote competitors, add their names to the list. For example, if you run a project management tool, you might add "Asana, Monday.com, Notion, ClickUp" to ensure your posts do not reference competing products.

### Sensitive topics

Add topics that are not appropriate for your brand or audience. This might include political topics, controversial subjects, or industry-specific sensitive areas.

### Overused buzzwords

If you want to avoid certain buzzwords or cliches that dilute your message, add them to the list. Examples: "synergy", "disrupt", "game-changer", "pivot".

### Personal preferences

Some creators have specific words or phrases they simply do not like. The Never Mention list lets you filter these out automatically.

## How the AI respects this list

When generating content, the AI checks your Never Mention list and actively avoids using any of the listed items. This applies to:

- Post generation
- Hook generation
- Idea generation
- AI Assist edits
- Content repurposing

The AI will find alternative words or rephrase sentences to avoid your blocked items while maintaining the message's intent.

## Tips

- **Start small** - Add only the items you truly want to avoid. Too many restrictions can limit content quality.
- **Be specific** - "Competitor X" is better than "competitors" (which would block all mentions of any competitor, even when relevant).
- **Review periodically** - Remove items that are no longer relevant and add new ones as needed.
- **Test the output** - After updating your list, generate a few posts to verify the changes take effect.
