## Overview

Network Notifications lets you create small groups of people whose LinkedIn posts you want to follow closely. When anyone in a group publishes a post via LinkedGrow, every other group member receives an email with a link straight to that post on LinkedIn.

The feature is an email notifier and nothing more than that. LinkedGrow does not like, comment, repost, or interact with the post on your behalf. If you want to engage, you click through to LinkedIn and engage there, in your own voice, on your own time - exactly as you would if a friend had texted you the link.

## Why this exists

LinkedIn's feed is noisy. If you follow a few hundred people, you regularly miss posts from the people you most care about - your peers, your network, the creators whose work you actually want to read.

A network group is just a small list of people whose posts you do not want to miss. The notification gets the post in front of you while it is fresh. What you do next is entirely up to you.

## Requirements

To use Network Notifications, every participant needs:

- A **LinkedIn account connected to LinkedGrow** (Dashboard > Settings > LinkedIn) - this is how LinkedGrow knows when they have published

## Creating a Group

1. Go to **Network Notifications** in the left sidebar
2. Click **New Group**
3. Enter a name (e.g. "Founder Circle", "Marketing Friends")
4. Click **Create**

You are automatically added as the owner.

## Inviting Members

1. Find your group and click **Invite**
2. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
3. Click **Send Invitation**

They receive an email with a link to join. They need an account on this instance to accept. Invitations expire after **7 days**. You can cancel a pending invitation at any time.

## What happens when someone publishes

When any group member publishes a LinkedIn post through LinkedGrow:

1. LinkedGrow detects the post has gone out
2. Every other group member receives an email titled "Someone just published a new post"
3. The email contains a short preview of the post and a button labeled **View on LinkedIn**
4. Clicking the button opens the post directly on LinkedIn

LinkedGrow never performs any action on LinkedIn on the recipient's behalf. No automated like, no automated comment, no automated repost. The recipient reads the post on LinkedIn and decides what (if anything) to do.

## Managing groups

As the group owner you can:

- **Rename** a group
- **Invite** new members
- **Remove members** you no longer want in the group
- **Cancel pending invitations**
- **Delete a group** to permanently remove it

Only the group owner can perform these actions.

## Important notes

- Network Notifications is a notification system, not an engagement system. LinkedGrow never likes, comments, or reposts on anyone's behalf.
- The "View on LinkedIn" link in the email opens the post directly on LinkedIn. Any engagement is up to you, manually, on LinkedIn, in your own voice.
- If you want to stop receiving notifications from a group, leave it.

## Questions?

Your instance administrator sets up the email provider these notifications go through, and anything broken belongs on the [LinkedGrow issue tracker](https://github.com/DigiHold/LinkedGrow/issues).
