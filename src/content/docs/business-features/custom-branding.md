## Overview

Custom branding lets you personalize your LinkedGrow carousels with your brand's visual identity. You can toggle your logo, set an avatar, add your handle and website, and choose brand colors - all from the Carousel Generator page.

Custom branding is available exclusively on the **Business plan**.

## Where to Find Branding Settings

Branding settings are located in the **Carousel Generator** page. When you open the carousel editor, the branding panel is available on the side where you can configure your brand elements for the carousel being created.

## Branding Panel Options

The branding panel in the Carousel Generator includes the following settings:

### Logo Toggle

Turn your logo on or off for the carousel. When enabled, your logo is displayed on the carousel slides.

### Avatar

Upload or select a profile avatar to display on your carousel slides. This is typically your headshot or company logo mark that appears alongside your content.

### Handle

Enter your social media handle (for example, your LinkedIn or X handle) to display on carousel slides. This helps viewers know who created the content.

### Website

Add your website URL to display on carousel slides. This drives traffic back to your site when people view your carousels.

### Primary Color

Your primary brand color is used as the main accent color in your carousel design. To set it:

1. Click on the **Primary Color** field
2. Enter a hex color code (for example, #0077B5) or use the color picker

### Secondary Color

Your secondary brand color is used for complementary design elements in your carousel. To set it:

1. Click on the **Secondary Color** field
2. Enter a hex color code or use the color picker

### Color Tips

- Choose colors with enough contrast for readability
- Your primary color should be your most recognizable brand color
- The secondary color works best as a complementary or accent color
- Avoid colors that are too light, as they may be hard to see on white backgrounds

## Where Branding Appears

Your custom branding is applied to **carousels** generated through the Carousel Generator. Branding settings affect the visual design of carousel slides, including color schemes, logo placement, and profile information.

Custom branding does not affect:

- Your LinkedGrow dashboard interface
- The post editor
- The content calendar
- Analytics views
- Your public LinkedIn profile or posts (LinkedIn controls its own styling)
- Emails sent by LinkedGrow (these use our standard template)

## Branding for Teams

When custom branding is configured on a team workspace:

- All team members see the same branding options in the Carousel Generator
- Only the team Owner can change branding settings
- Members with the Admin or Member role can use the existing branding but cannot modify it
