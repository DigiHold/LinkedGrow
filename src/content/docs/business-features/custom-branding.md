## Overview

Custom branding lets you personalize your LinkedGrow workspace with your company's visual identity. You can upload your logo, set brand colors, and choose fonts to create a consistent branded experience across your content workflow.

Custom branding is available exclusively on the **Business plan**.

## Setting Up Custom Branding

To configure your brand settings:

1. Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar
2. Click on the **Branding** tab
3. Configure your brand elements (logo, colors, fonts)
4. Click **Save Changes**

Changes take effect immediately across your workspace.

## Logo Upload

Upload your company or personal brand logo to display throughout your LinkedGrow workspace:

1. In the Branding settings, find the **Logo** section
2. Click **Upload Logo**
3. Select an image file from your computer
4. Adjust the crop area if needed
5. Click **Save**

Logo requirements:

- **Formats supported:** PNG, JPG, SVG, or WebP
- **Recommended size:** at least 200x200 pixels
- **Maximum file size:** 2 MB
- **Transparent backgrounds** work best for a clean look

Your logo appears in the top left corner of the dashboard and in team-related areas. If you are using team collaboration, all team members see the team's branded logo.

## Brand Colors

Set your primary and secondary brand colors to customize the look of your workspace:

### Primary Color

Your primary brand color is used for:

- Action buttons and interactive elements
- Active navigation highlights
- Progress indicators
- Links within the editor

To set your primary color:

1. In the Branding settings, find the **Colors** section
2. Click on the **Primary Color** field
3. Enter a hex color code (for example, #0077B5) or use the color picker
4. The preview updates in real time

### Secondary Color

Your secondary brand color is used for:

- Secondary buttons
- Background accents
- Card borders and highlights
- Status indicators

To set your secondary color:

1. Click on the **Secondary Color** field
2. Enter a hex color code or use the color picker

### Color Tips

- Choose colors with enough contrast against white and dark backgrounds (LinkedGrow supports both light and dark mode)
- Your primary color should be your most recognizable brand color
- The secondary color works best as a complementary or accent color
- Avoid colors that are too light, as they may be hard to see on white backgrounds

## Font Family

Choose a font that matches your brand's typography:

1. In the Branding settings, find the **Typography** section
2. Click the **Font Family** dropdown
3. Browse the available fonts or search by name
4. Select your preferred font
5. The preview updates to show how the font looks

Available font options include a curated selection of popular web fonts that are optimized for readability in a content creation interface.

The selected font is applied to:

- Post editor text
- Preview panels
- Dashboard headings
- Content cards

**Note:** The font selection applies to your LinkedGrow workspace only. It does not affect how your posts appear on LinkedIn, since LinkedIn applies its own typography to published content.

## Where Branding Appears

Your custom branding is visible in:

- **Dashboard** - logo and colors throughout the interface
- **Post editor** - branded color accents and selected font
- **Content calendar** - branded event colors
- **Team workspace** - all team members see the branded experience
- **Analytics views** - branded chart colors

Custom branding does not appear on:

- Your public LinkedIn profile or posts (LinkedIn controls its own styling)
- Emails sent by LinkedGrow (these use our standard template)
- The sign-in page (this uses the default LinkedGrow branding)

## Resetting to Defaults

If you want to remove your custom branding and return to the default LinkedGrow look:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Branding**
2. Click **Reset to Defaults**
3. Confirm the reset

This removes your logo, resets colors to LinkedGrow defaults, and restores the default font. You can set up custom branding again at any time.

## Branding for Teams

When custom branding is configured on a team workspace:

- All team members see the branded interface
- Only the team Owner and Admins can change branding settings
- Members with the Member role see the branding but cannot modify it

If you manage multiple teams, each team can have its own unique branding configuration.
