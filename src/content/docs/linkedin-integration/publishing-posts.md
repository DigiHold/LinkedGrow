## How to publish a post

Once your LinkedIn account is connected, publishing is straightforward:

1. Create or edit a post in the **Editor**
2. Review the content and check the Algorithm Score
3. Click **Publish Now** at the bottom of the editor
4. LinkedGrow writes the post into LinkedIn's own composer using your connected session
5. The post appears on your profile, usually within a couple of minutes

It is a couple of minutes rather than a couple of seconds because the post is
typed rather than sent through an API, at a pace that looks like a person
writing it. The editor tells you where it is up to, and you can leave the page:
the post finishes on its own and shows up under Posts.

After publishing, the post status changes to "Published" in your Posts library,
with a link to the post itself.

## Supported post formats

### Text posts

The most common format. Write your content in the editor and publish. Text posts support up to 3,000 characters on LinkedIn.

### Image posts

Attach a single image to your post. The image can be uploaded from your device or generated with AI (Pro+ plan). The editor supports attaching one image at a time.

### Video posts

You can attach a video file to your post. Videos have to be published immediately
rather than scheduled, because LinkedGrow does not keep your video file: it is
passed straight through to LinkedIn and deleted. Video uploads also take the
longest of any format, so give it a few minutes.

## Verifying your post went live

LinkedGrow checks for you: after publishing it looks for the post on your own
profile and stores the link, and it only marks a post published once it has
found it. If it cannot find it, the post is still marked published and carries a
note asking you to check, because posting the same thing twice is worse than an
uncertain link.

You can also verify it yourself by:

- Checking the post status in the **Posts** library (should show "Published" with a timestamp)
- Opening LinkedIn directly to see the post on your profile
- Checking the "Recent Posts" section on your dashboard

## Failed posts

Occasionally a post may fail to publish. Common reasons include:

- **Your LinkedIn session has expired** - Sign in once from Settings and the post goes out on its own
- **LinkedIn asked for a verification code** - The same fix: sign in once and confirm the code
- **An attachment did not finish uploading** - Most often a large video on a slow connection
- **LinkedIn changed its composer** - Rare, and we fix it centrally when it happens

LinkedGrow tries three times before giving up. A post that fails all three shows
a "Failed" status in the Posts library with the reason on it, and it is never
silently dropped. You can edit and retry publishing from the editor.

## Best practices

- **Review before publishing** - AI-generated content is a first draft. Always read through and make personal edits before hitting Publish
- **Check the Algorithm Score** - Aim for 70+ for best engagement potential
- **Preview on mobile** - Most LinkedIn users browse on phones, so make sure your formatting looks good on small screens
- **Time your posts** - If you are publishing immediately, consider whether your audience is currently active on LinkedIn
- **Monitor after publishing** - Engage with comments on your post within the first hour for maximum algorithm boost
