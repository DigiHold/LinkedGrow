## What is the Ideas Generator?

The Ideas Generator helps you overcome content block by providing AI-powered post ideas tailored to your niche. Instead of staring at a blank screen wondering what to write about, you can browse a list of curated ideas and turn any of them into a full post with one click.

## How to use it

1. Click **Ideas** in the sidebar
2. Choose a theme from 10 popular options:
   - Productivity, Leadership, AI & Tech, Career Growth, Startups, Remote Work, Marketing, Sales, Entrepreneurship, Personal Branding
3. Or enter a custom theme for more specific results
4. Click **Generate Ideas**

The AI produces 10 content ideas. Each idea includes:

- **Main hook or concept** - A specific angle or topic you can develop into a full post
- **Post type** - The suggested format (Story, Advice, List, Opinion, Case Study)
- **Category** - How the idea is classified
- **Engagement prediction** - An estimate of how well the topic typically performs on LinkedIn, shown as a color-coded badge:
  - Green = Very High engagement potential
  - Blue = High engagement potential
  - Gray = Medium engagement potential

## Turning ideas into posts

When you find an idea you like, click the **Create Post** button. A confirmation dialog appears, and after confirming, the AI generates a complete LinkedIn post based on that idea. The post opens in the editor where you can refine it before publishing.

This two-step process (idea then full post) helps you maintain editorial control. You pick the direction, and the AI handles the writing.

## Building a content pipeline

Use the Ideas Generator strategically to plan ahead:

1. **Weekly brainstorm** - Spend 10 minutes generating 20-30 ideas at the start of each week
2. **Select your best 5** - Pick the ideas with the highest engagement predictions that align with your goals
3. **Generate and schedule** - Turn each idea into a post and schedule them across the week
4. **Mix themes** - Alternate between educational, personal, and promotional content

This approach gives you a full week of content in under an hour.

## Tips for better ideas

- **Be specific with your theme.** "B2B SaaS growth strategies" produces better ideas than just "marketing".
- **Consider your audience.** Choose themes that align with what your target audience cares about.
- **Use engagement predictions as a guide.** Higher-predicted ideas often tap into trending topics or proven LinkedIn formats.
- **Combine ideas.** Sometimes the best post comes from merging two related ideas into one.

## Availability

The Ideas Generator is available to every user of the instance, and post generation from ideas runs on the AI provider you configured in Settings.
