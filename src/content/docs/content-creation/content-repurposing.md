## What is content repurposing?

Content repurposing lets you transform existing content from other platforms into LinkedIn posts. Instead of creating everything from scratch, you can take a blog article, YouTube video, or Reddit thread and have the AI extract key insights and format them for LinkedIn.

## Supported content sources

### Articles and blog posts

Paste any article URL and LinkedGrow will:

1. Fetch and read the article content
2. Extract the main points, insights, and takeaways
3. Generate a LinkedIn post that captures the essence in an engaging format

This works with blog posts, news articles, industry reports, and most web pages with readable content.

### YouTube videos

Paste a YouTube URL and LinkedGrow will:

1. Fetch the video transcript
2. Identify the key insights and talking points
3. Create a LinkedIn post summarizing the most valuable content

This is especially useful for repurposing your own video content, podcast episodes, or conference talks into written LinkedIn posts.

### Reddit threads

Paste a Reddit URL to fetch the thread and its top comments. The AI analyzes the discussion and creates LinkedIn-appropriate content that captures the most interesting perspectives and insights.

## How to use it

1. Click **Repurpose** in the sidebar (or the "Repurpose Content" quick action on the dashboard)
2. Paste the URL of the content you want to repurpose
3. The tool identifies the content type (article, YouTube, Reddit) automatically
4. Click **Generate**
5. The AI creates a LinkedIn post based on the source content
6. Review and edit in the post editor

You can also generate an AI image to accompany the repurposed post if you have image generation configured (Pro+ plan).

## Tips for effective repurposing

- **Add your perspective.** The best repurposed posts are not just summaries - they include your unique take on the source material.
- **Give credit.** If you are repurposing someone else's content, mention the original source in your post.
- **Choose high-quality sources.** The AI output is only as good as the input. Pick articles and videos with strong, clear insights.
- **Repurpose your own content.** Blog posts you have written, talks you have given, and podcasts you have recorded are perfect candidates.
- **Adapt the format.** A 2,000-word article might become a list post, a story, or a series of posts - experiment with different angles.
