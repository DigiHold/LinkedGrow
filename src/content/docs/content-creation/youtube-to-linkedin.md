## What is YouTube to LinkedIn?

YouTube to LinkedIn lets you turn any YouTube video into a LinkedIn post. The AI extracts key insights from the video transcript and rewrites them as a standalone LinkedIn post in your writing voice.

This is useful when you watch industry content - interviews, conference talks, tutorials, breakdowns - and want to share the insights with your LinkedIn audience without just dropping a link that the algorithm will bury.

## How to use it

### Step 1 - Paste a YouTube URL

Go to **Repurpose** in the sidebar and paste any YouTube video URL. LinkedGrow automatically detects it as a YouTube source and pulls the video transcript.

Both `youtube.com/watch?v=...` and `youtu.be/...` short links work.

### Step 2 - Choose a hook

The AI analyzes the transcript and generates multiple hooks - different angles you could take for your LinkedIn post. Each hook highlights a different insight or argument from the video. Pick the one that fits the message you want to share.

### Step 3 - Pick a post variation

Based on your selected hook, the AI generates 3 post variations. Each one takes a different approach - maybe one is a story format, another is a list, another is a hot take. Select the one you like best.

### Step 4 - Edit and publish

The selected post opens in the editor where you can refine the text, attach images, save as draft, schedule for later, or publish immediately to LinkedIn.

## Tips for YouTube repurposing

- **One video, multiple posts.** A 30-minute video can give you 2-3 completely different LinkedIn posts by pulling different threads from it.
- **Add your own take.** The best repurposed posts include your perspective on the video's content, not just a summary of what was said.
- **Credit the creator.** Mention the video creator in your post - it builds goodwill and they might engage with your post.
- **Works with any video that has a transcript.** The AI needs a transcript to work with, so videos without captions or auto-generated subtitles may not produce results.
