## What is Blog to LinkedIn?

Blog to LinkedIn lets you turn any blog article into a native LinkedIn post. Instead of sharing a link that the algorithm deprioritizes, you create a standalone post that delivers value on its own - keeping your audience on the platform and getting better distribution.

## Supported blog platforms

LinkedGrow automatically detects blog articles from these platforms:

- Medium
- Substack
- dev.to
- Hashnode
- WordPress.com
- Ghost
- Beehiiv
- Mirror

Any URL with `/blog/`, `/post/`, `/article/`, `/posts/`, `/articles/`, `/p/`, or `/note/` in the path is also detected as a blog article.

## How to use it

### Step 1 - Paste a blog URL

Go to **Repurpose** in the sidebar and paste the URL of the blog article. LinkedGrow detects it as a blog source and extracts the article content.

### Step 2 - Choose a hook

The AI reads the article and generates multiple hooks - different angles for your LinkedIn post. One might focus on the main argument, another on a surprising statistic, another on a personal lesson. Pick the angle that fits your audience.

### Step 3 - Pick a post variation

Based on your hook, the AI generates 3 post variations with different formats and approaches. Select the one you want to work with.

### Step 4 - Edit and publish

The post opens in the editor where you can refine, add images, save as draft, schedule, or publish immediately.

## Tips for blog repurposing

- **Repurpose your own articles.** Blog posts you spent hours writing deserve more than one audience. Turn each article into 2-3 LinkedIn posts pulling different angles.
- **Don't just summarize.** The best LinkedIn posts from blog articles extract one specific insight and expand on it with your personal experience or opinion.
- **Drop the link in the first comment.** If you want to drive traffic to the original article, put the URL in a comment after publishing rather than in the post body. Links in the body reduce reach.
- **One article, multiple posts.** A long article can become a contrarian take, a how-to list, a personal story, and a framework post - each reaching a different segment of your audience.
