## Blog Agent Reference

**IMPORTANT: Reference `/Users/nicolas/Agent/Blog` for the full blog writing system.**

The blog agent at `/Users/nicolas/Agent/Blog` contains the complete automated workflow for writing and publishing articles. When writing blog content for LinkedGrow, follow the same quality standards:

### Writing Quality Standards (from Blog Agent)

- **2,500+ words minimum** per article (beat #1 competitor by 500-1,000 words)
- **Sound human, not AI** - write like explaining to a WordPress expert friend
- Use 2-4 sentence paragraphs with natural rhythm
- Vary sentence length naturally (8-30 words typical range)
- Use contractions and personal pronouns
- Include specific details, real examples, tool names
- NO em dashes (use periods, commas, or semicolons)
- NO sentence fragments for dramatic effect
- **MAXIMUM 3-4 statistics in the ENTIRE article** - count them, if more than 4 you MUST cut
- Most paragraphs should have ZERO statistics - use practical explanations instead
- NEVER write "According to [source]" more than once - ideally zero times
- NEVER stack multiple stats in the same paragraph - pick ONE or explain without numbers
- NEVER use "research shows", "studies found", "data reveals", "reports indicate" - these are research paper phrases, not blog writing
- **The research phase is for YOUR knowledge, not the reader's** - internalize the data, then write from understanding, not from citations
- Explain through experience and logic, not stat dumps - the article is a conversation, not a thesis

### Article Structure (from Blog Agent)

- **Introduction**: 300-400 words before first H2 (flowing paragraphs, no lists)
- **Main sections**: 4-6 sections, each 400-600 words with H2 heading + image
- **Each section**: 4-6 substantial paragraphs (no bare lists)
- **Practical section**: Step-by-step allowed but with 2-3 paragraphs per step
- **Closing**: Unique title (NOT "Conclusion"), 150-200 words, clear takeaway
- H2 for main sections, H3 for subsections (NO H1 in content - page title is H1)

### Image Generation (from Blog Agent)

- Use **Google Gemini 3 Pro Image** (Nano Banana Pro) for article images
- Featured image: represents entire article, 16:9 aspect ratio, photorealistic
- Content images: one per H2 section (except last section)
- Generate EXTREMELY detailed prompts (300+ words each) for best results
- Convert to AVIF format using Sharp (quality 60, effort 9) for 40-70KB files
- All images need descriptive alt text with relevant keywords
- Variety of visual approaches - NOT repetitive patterns

### AI Detection Target

- Target: 30% or lower AI detection score
- Avoid systematic patterns, documentation tone, stat dumps
- Use the review process to iterate until passing quality threshold

### Internal Linking (adapted for LinkedGrow)

- 5+ relevant internal links per article
- Mix links in intro, body, and closing sections
- Use descriptive anchor text (NOT "click here")
- Link to: feature pages, audience pages, free tools, related blog posts
- Only link when genuinely helpful to the reader

### External Backlinks

- **1-2 external links per article** to authoritative sources (research, official docs, industry reports)
- **NEVER link to competitors or SaaS tools that sell similar features** (no Taplio, PostUnreel, AuthoredUp, Supergrow, ContentIn, Socialsonic, aiCarousels, PostNitro, etc.). Linking to a competitor gives them SEO juice and sends our readers to their product. Use non-competitor sources instead: analytics platforms (Dataslayer, Agorapulse blog), research firms, official LinkedIn docs, general marketing blogs (Buffer, HubSpot, Hootsuite are acceptable as general social media platforms).
- Use `<a href="..." target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">` with cyan link styling
- Only cite statistics that can be verified by fetching the source URL
- Keep it minimal - more than 2 feels spammy and sends readers away
